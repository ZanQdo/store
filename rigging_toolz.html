Time Saver

Edit Custom Shapes Instantly

No more digging through widget collections! In Pose Mode, select any bone and click "Edit Custom Shape". The addon instantly converts it to a temporary mesh, puts you in Edit Mode, and handles the matrix math. Edit your shape, click again, and snap right back to posing.

Weighting Tools

1-Click Skinning Environment

Hit "Toggle Weight Paint" to instantly jump into the perfect setup. It automatically enables Armature X-Ray, shows mesh wireframes, isolates the character, and solos your deform bones. Hit it again to return exactly to your previous state.

Batch Operations

Global Rig Toggles

Instantly boost viewport performance by toggling heavy modifiers (Subsurf, Cloth, Multires) across your entire character. Easily batch-change rotation modes, reset constraint distances, or set B-Bone thickness across the whole rig.

Rigify Revolution

Safely edit your final rig and regenerate without losing work.

The old workflow forced you to lose custom edits. Now, freely add constraints, drivers, and custom shapes directly to the generated rig. When you need to update your metarig and regenerate, Pataz Rigging Toolz intelligently reapplies all your custom work.

The Cleanup Crew

Sanitize your models to prevent export errors and bloat. Works on all selected objects simultaneously.

Relax Topology

Smooth mesh topology efficiently while preserving overall volume. Operates seamlessly in Edit or Object mode. This is perfect for quickly evening out dense areas after sculpting or boolean operations without destroying the original silhouette of your character.

Clean Vertex Groups

Normalize, remove zero weights, and enforce group limits across multiple meshes at once. Crucial for keeping game engine exports clean and performant.

Purge Dead Data

One-click operators to safely clear UV maps, custom normals, sculpt masks, and color attributes that are inflating file sizes or causing shading artifacts.

Shape Key Management

Quickly wipe out all shape keys, or apply current shape key mixes to the base mesh permanently across all selected objects.
