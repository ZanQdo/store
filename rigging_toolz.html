The Rigify Revolution

A truly non-destructive generation workflow.

Standard Rigify is destructive—if you edit the generated rig, you lose your work upon regeneration. Pataz Rigging Toolz changes everything. Freely add custom constraints, shape drivers, and complex properties directly to the generated rig. When you update your metarig and hit "Generate", all your custom work is intelligently preserved and reapplied!

TIME SAVER

Edit Custom Shapes Instantly

No more digging through widget collections! In Pose Mode, select any bone and click "Edit Custom Shape". The addon instantly converts it to a temporary mesh, puts you in Edit Mode, and handles the matrix math. Edit your shape, click again, and snap right back to posing.

WEIGHTING TOOLS

1-Click Skinning Environment

Hit "Toggle Weight Paint" to instantly jump into the perfect setup. It automatically enables Armature X-Ray, shows mesh wireframes, isolates the character, and solos your deform bones.

Global Modifier Toggles

Experiencing viewport lag while animating? Instantly turn off heavy modifiers (Subdivision, Cloth, Multires) across every mesh attached to your rig simultaneously.

Batch Rigging Operations

Perform sweeping changes with single clicks. Change Rotation Modes (Euler/Quaternion) globally, scale all B-Bones for visibility, or safely clear poses on protected layers.

The Cleanup Crew

Sanitize your models to prevent export errors and bloat. Works on all selected objects simultaneously.

Relax Topology

Smooth mesh topology efficiently while preserving overall volume. Operates seamlessly in Edit or Object mode. This is perfect for quickly evening out dense areas after sculpting or boolean operations without destroying the original silhouette of your character.

Clean Vertex Groups

Normalize, remove zero weights, and enforce group limits across multiple meshes at once. Crucial for keeping game engine exports clean and performant.

Purge Dead Data

One-click removal of unused shape keys, old UV maps, custom split normals, and leftover skin modifier data that clutters your outliner.

Smart Weight Transfer

Auto-transfer weights between meshes. Intelligently attempts a topology match first, automatically falling back to nearest-face interpolation.
