Global & Partial Snapshots

Isolate your experiments. Create snapshots of individual materials, node trees, actions, or objects without ballooning your project's file size.

Material & Shader States Save iterations of complex node networks directly from the Shader Editor.

Animation Iteration

Restore the exact keyframes of a specific bone or a set of F-Curves without losing the progress you've made on the rest of the rig.

❖
Pinpoint Channel Recovery Select individual transform channels in the Graph Editor—like a specific axis of rotation—and restore only those curves.
❖
Ghost Curves Support Project past iterations directly into the Graph Editor as Ghost Curves for precise visual comparison before committing.
❖
Bulletproof Drivers Safely revert complex driver variables, targets, and expressions without breaking rig relationships.
